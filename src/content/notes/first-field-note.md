This is a draft/example note, not a publication. The point of an experiment is not only to obtain a result; it is to leave enough of a trail that a future reader can understand what was run, why it was run, and what the result does not establish.

For the systems work I care about, that trail begins with pinned inputs and an executable path. The useful question is rarely “does this look plausible?” It is “can this be inspected, repeated, and revised without relying on memory?”
